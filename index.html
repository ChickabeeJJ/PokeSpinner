How to Play

  1. Spin the wheel, then hit LOCK to land on a rarity. Better balls tilt the wheel toward rare Pokémon.
  2. Throw your ball when the ring is small and green for the best catch odds.
  3. Build your Team of up to 6 and check its type coverage.
  4. Beat trainers, gym leaders, rivals and villains on the Adventure map to earn coins, badges and new regions.
  5. Fill the Pokédex to claim milestone rewards, and stock up at the Poké Mart (look for the Daily Deal!).
  6. Check the weather under the title: it changes every 3 hours, brings out certain types and powers up their moves.
  7. Finish the Daily Quests (map button, top right) for coins, Apricorns and a daily chest.
  8. Visit the Safari Zone from Adventure, and turn Apricorns into balls and gear at Kurt's Workshop in the Mart.
  9. Shiny Pokémon appear at full odds, 1/4096. Good luck!
